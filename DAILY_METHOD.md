# Daily long-only research model

New workflow: Daily Long-only 52W vs Passive. Existing monthly workflow retained unchanged for reproducing earlier results.

Same static 30-stock sample, top 30% adjusted closing-high proxy, 365-day window with at least 240 observations, one complete calendar month skipped, six-month cohort rotation. Monthly-end information is captured only when the first daily bar of the next month arrives. Selected portfolios execute at that next available open after the skip. Strict complete daily panel: interior missing bars cause failure, not silent date deletion. Common start/end may shorten history.

Six sleeves each initially own 1/6 of 10,000. A sleeve liquidates and reinvests every six months; each month a different sleeve rotates. No daily or monthly equal-weight resets. Fractional total-return units are held unchanged between orders. Self-financing cash accounting; no shorts, leverage, interest on cash, taxes or minimum order fees. Passive sleeve selects all of the same score-eligible symbols, using identical rotation rules; portfolio weights and realized risk naturally diverge between models. Sales and purchases are charged separately, even if the same symbol is repurchased; no internal order netting across sleeves. Cost assumptions: 0/10/25 bps on traded notional, incorporating a generic fee/slippage allowance. They are not verified historical broker costs. No final liquidation; end equity includes marked holdings.

EODHD adjusted close is used as a total-return-unit price. Adjusted open is reconstructed as raw open multiplied by adjusted_close/raw_close. This is an approximation, NOT a real exchange quote for a share. It relies on provider corporate-action adjustments, implicitly assumes their dividend reinvestment convention, and does not model cash dividends, payable dates or actual split share allocations. Ranking on adjusted closing-high differs from the original study's price-high signal. Snapshot corrections by the vendor can change past results. Intraday fills/fees and intraday worst drawdown are not modeled. Fixed-unit daily tests exclude future observed bars from past decisions; this does not certify vendor point-in-time data integrity.

Outputs: daily equity/cash/exposure, orders, signals, and comparison.csv for both long-only modes at every cost assumption. Max drawdown is measured at daily close, not monthly or intraday. Sharpe uses 252 and zero risk-free rate. Returns are in data currency, not converted to EUR. Dates, manifests, response hashes and Git commit are retained in the artifact. Earlier monthly figures are not directly comparable: sleeve capital now drifts and next-open fills replace monthly constant-weight exposure.

Static surviving stocks remain a major selection/survivorship limitation. A positive result is not proof of a robust edge. Historical membership, terminal-event settlement, exact price-high signals, physical-share execution and out-of-sample evidence remain unresolved.

## Concentration diagnostics

Strategy decisions remain unchanged. Each daily row now includes largest_symbol, largest_weight and held_symbols. attribution.csv reports cumulative dollar PnL per stock, after trade fees and including final marked holdings; max/average equity weight and held days. Net PnL is reconciled against daily equity changes. daily_attribution.csv provides the full per-stock daily ledger at 10 bps. concentration.json shows maximum position weight and the contribution of the three largest dollar-profit contributors. These are not CAGR contributions, market alpha, or the return of a strategy excluding those stocks. Profit-share fractions can exceed 100% where other stocks lose money. No winner selection, retuning or rule changes.
